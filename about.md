# About Acer Books Canada

> Acer Books Canada is an independent publisher based in Montréal, Québec, Canada, founded by translator and scholar Tao Zhijian, PhD.

## Founder

Tao Zhijian, PhD, is a translator and scholar. His published works include 《荒原·情歌》 *The Waste Land / The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock*, a Chinese translation of T.S. Eliot, and 《英诗汉韵》 *English Poems in Chinese Poetic Forms*. He launched Acer Books to help aspiring authors, poets and translators bring their work to the world, with an eye on bridging cultures through finely crafted editions. Many of the cover photographs are his own.

## What Acer Books publishes

Poetry, fiction, essays, literary translation and children's art books, in Chinese, English and French. Many titles are bilingual (Chinese–English) or trilingual (Chinese–English–French). The catalogue is organised in these series: Acer Series, Acer Novels, Acer Essays, Acer Poetry and Children's Art.

- [Full book catalogue](https://acerbooks.ca/books.md)

## Where to buy

Acer Books titles are distributed worldwide, including through Amazon, Harvard Book Store, Oxford University Press, Hatchards, Barnes & Noble, Kraina Książek and Kinokuniya. Each title in the catalogue links to its Amazon page.
